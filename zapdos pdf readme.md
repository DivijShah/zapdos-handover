The call that ends at night.
6pm in Austin, a warehouse lead wants a camera layout and a price by Thursday, plus their site manager on the
next call. Austin logs off. Singapore wakes up. Does the AI already know what's owed, to whom and by when? Has it
handed each piece to the right person: the layout to the engineer, the price to the founder, the invite to whoever
runs the call? And has it finished what it can on its own?
The double-booked afternoon.

day before.

again.
